# Privacy Policy for CleanTab

**Effective Date:** October 24, 2026  
**Last Updated:** October 24, 2026  

CleanTab ("we", "our", or "the Extension") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding user data, permissions, and security.

---

### 1. Zero Data Collection
CleanTab does **not** collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information, browsing history, keystrokes, IP addresses, or analytics. 

* **No Analytics or Telemetry:** CleanTab contains no tracking pixels, Google Analytics, or third-party tracking scripts.
* **No External Servers:** The Extension has no remote server backend and does not transmit data outside your local browser environment.
* **100% Client-Side:** Everything executes exclusively on your machine.

---

### 2. Browser Permissions Justification
CleanTab requests only the minimal set of permissions required to deliver its core functionality:

* **`bookmarks`**: Used exclusively to read and display your bookmarks hierarchy in the slide-out bookmarks drawer, and to allow local bookmark management (such as deleting a bookmark). No bookmark information is ever transmitted or shared.
* **`favicon`**: Used to load and display website icons alongside bookmarks in the bookmarks manager to aid visual navigation.

---

### 3. Local Storage Usage
All user configurations—including custom search engines, quick search aliases, local search history for autocomplete, keyboard shortcuts, and theme preferences—are saved strictly within your browser's local storage (`chrome.storage.local`). This data never leaves your device and can be cleared at any time directly through the extension's settings or by clearing your browser data.

---

### 4. Remote Code
CleanTab complies strictly with Chrome Web Store Manifest V3 policies. The Extension does **not** use, load, or execute any remote code or external scripts. All HTML, CSS, and JavaScript files are packaged and served locally from the extension bundle.

---

### 5. Third-Party Web Searches
When you submit a search query, CleanTab routes your query directly to your selected search engine (such as Google, DuckDuckGo, Brave, Bing, Yandex, or Ecosia) by opening their public search URL in your browser. These requests are subject to the privacy policies of the respective search engine providers.

---

### 6. Changes to this Policy
If we make changes to this Privacy Policy, the updated version will be posted in this repository with an updated revision date.
